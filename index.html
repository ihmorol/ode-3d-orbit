Lorenz (1960), Tellus 12, 243-254 2D barotropic vorticity

A 3-wave weather model: ∇²ψ = A cos ly + F cos kx + 2G sin ly sin kx. Energy E and vorticity V stay exactly constant, so the orbits are closed curves where the two ellipsoids meet.
